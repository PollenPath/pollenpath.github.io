How it works

A clear process from opportunity to delivery

01

Opportunity identified

We identify funding or procurement opportunities relevant to your type of organisation.

02

We speak with you

We explain the opportunity, confirm key requirements and outline the documentation support available.

03

Information collected

You provide the organisation, project and supporting information required for the specific opportunity.

04

Documentation prepared

Your documents are prepared around your organisation and the actual requirements of the opportunity.

05

Quality review

We check the documents for accuracy, consistency, requirements and presentation before delivery.

06

You submit

You receive the completed documentation and remain responsible for submitting the application directly.
